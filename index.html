About Us

Women Artists In Collections And Museums (WAICAM) is a passion-project database dedicated to documenting and highlighting the work of women artists held, exhibited, and represented by museums and cultural institutions around the world.

Based in California and currently run by two people, WAICAM is a growing, community-driven project. We welcome anyone interested in helping us research and document artworks, collections, and exhibitions featuring women artists.

Our research on permanent collections and past exhibitions is conducted and documented entirely by humans. At present, we use AI-assisted tools to help gather and organize information for exhibitions and collections that are frequently updated. However, our long-term goal is to transition toward a primarily human-led process, with volunteers helping expand the database.

If you have suggestions, corrections, resources, or would like to contribute to the project, please contact us.
